Public sector2023–2024

Commonwealth of Pennsylvania

Residents had to know which agency owned their task before they could find it, the opposite of the governor's "No wrong door" commitment.

Associate Creative Director, Experience · Publicis Sapient

  • My part

    I owned the UX architecture and led the design team. In workshops with each agency, I reorganized their sites around how residents look for content and take it in. I also set the content strategy, replacing folder-based organization with tagging so search could filter the way residents think, including by life event.

  • What happened

    PA.gov launched in phases on that architecture, completing in January 2025. Agencies weren't required to adopt the new structure, and all but a handful did.

  • 43 agencies
  • 37% → 95% accessibility
  • ~9M visits in December 2024

Grupo Coppel

Coppel's leadership wanted design work on par with the best tech companies, and set up a Digital Center of Excellence to get there. I joined as a Senior UX Strategist and a founding member.

Digital Center of Excellence · Publicis Sapient

  • My part

    I ran a skills-gap assessment against that bar, then rewrote most of the design organization's job descriptions around quality and mentoring, with clear criteria for hiring and promotion. I also found that no one owned the journey across six phase-based pods, and led an operating-model proposal to fix it.

  • What happened

    The new job descriptions were broadly agreed on, and my criteria were used to hire new senior managers, with me on the interview panel. Leadership accepted the structural finding but deferred the reorganization behind a CEO-led redesign. The design principles I facilitated stuck.

  • 35-person design org
  • 14-month engagement
  • Offered a direct role
Industrial B2B · New product · AI~6 months

Visualization tool

Contractors needed to show homeowners how real roofing and siding would look on their house, then turn that choice straight into an order.

Associate Creative Director, Experience · Publicis Sapient

  • My part

    I led one to three designers from first research to final sprint. Contractor research showed that rendering quality mattered more than real-time speed, so I used Figma Make prototypes to make the case for generative-AI rendering.

  • The lesson

    The evidence reached the decision-maker after real-time development had shipped, and the client held course. Next time I'll test the riskiest assumptions before the build commits.

How I work

Build shared ground before arguing structure

People who disagree about structure usually disagree about who they're serving. I settle that first, then let the group reach the structure together.

Earn adoption instead of requiring it

Most of my best work had no mandate behind it. Agencies, brands, and teams adopted it because it held up for them.

Keep making the thing

I still prototype, and I use AI daily to move faster: testing interactions in Figma Make, drafting standards and documentation, exploring options before a team commits.
