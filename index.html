Public sector2023–2024

One front door for 43 state agencies

Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, PA.gov

UX Lead (Associate Creative Director, Experience) · Publicis Sapient

  • The problem

    Pennsylvania had 62 websites organized around the state's org chart. A resident had to know which agency owned a benefit before they could even start looking for it.

  • My part

    I owned the UX architecture and core sitemap, organizing the whole estate around 21 life events instead of agencies. I led the design team and ran IA workshops with each agency.

  • What happened

    The new PA.gov launched in January 2025 on that architecture. The agencies had no obligation to adopt the IA, and all but a handful did.

  • 62 → 1 sites
  • 21 life events
  • 37% → 95% accessibility score
  • ~9M visits in Dec 2024
Visit pa.gov

Outcome figures are for the full program. "No wrong door" was the Governor's commitment; my job was turning it into a structure people could navigate.

Retail & banking · Design operations14 months

Advising the leaders of a 35-person design organization

Grupo Coppel, Digital Center of Excellence

Senior UX Strategist · Publicis Sapient

  • The problem

    Six product pods each owned one phase of the shopping funnel. Nobody owned the path between them, and design was in the room but downstream of the decisions.

  • My part

    I tested the diagnosis with product and engineering first, then led an operating-model proposal: channel teams co-led by product and design, a guild to own the end-to-end journey, and clear KPI and deadline ownership.

  • What happened

    Leadership accepted the finding and deferred the reorganization behind a CEO-led redesign. The design principles I facilitated stuck, and designers were still citing them months later.

  • 35-person design org
  • Engagement extended to 14 months
  • Offered a direct role
Industrial B2B · New product · AI~6 months

A product bet, and what I'd do differently

ABC Supply, home exterior visualizer

Associate Creative Director, Experience · Publicis Sapient

  • The product

    A free tool contractors use with homeowners: upload a photo of the house, try real roofing and siding from the catalog, and send the choice straight into an order.

  • My part

    I led one to three designers from first research to final sprint. Contractor research said quality mattered more than real-time, so I used Figma Make prototypes to make the case for a generative-AI rendering approach.

  • The lesson

    The evidence reached the decision-maker after real-time development had shipped, and the client held course. Next time I'll get the riskiest assumptions tested before the build commits.

How I work

Build shared ground before arguing structure

People who disagree about structure usually disagree about who they're serving. I settle that first, then let the group reach the structure together.

Earn adoption instead of requiring it

Most of my best work had no mandate behind it. Agencies, brands, and teams adopted it because it held up for them.

Keep making the thing

I still prototype, and I use AI daily to move faster: testing interactions in Figma Make, drafting standards and documentation, exploring options before a team commits.
