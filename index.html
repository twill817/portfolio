Public sector2023–2024

Commonwealth of Pennsylvania

Residents had to know which agency owned their task before they could find it, the opposite of the governor's "No wrong door" commitment.

Associate Creative Director, Experience · Publicis Sapient

  • My part

    I owned the UX architecture and led the design team. In workshops with each agency, I reorganized their sites around how residents look for content and take it in. I also set the content strategy, replacing folder-based organization with tagging so search could filter the way residents think, including by life event.

  • What happened

    PA.gov launched in phases on that architecture, completing in January 2025. Agencies weren't required to adopt the new structure, and all but a handful did.

  • 43 agencies
  • 37% → 95% accessibility audit score
  • ~9M visits in December 2024

Grupo Coppel

Coppel's leadership wanted design work on par with the best tech companies, and set up a Digital Center of Excellence to get there. I joined as a founding member.

Senior UX Strategist · Publicis Sapient

  • My part

    I ran a skills-gap assessment against that bar, then rewrote most of the design organization's job descriptions around quality and mentoring, with clear criteria for hiring and promotion. I also found that no one owned the journey across six phase-based pods, and led an operating-model proposal to fix it.

  • What happened

    The new job descriptions were broadly agreed on, and my criteria were used to hire new senior managers, with me on the interview panel. The design principles I facilitated are still in Coppel's design system, which credits me for leading them. Leadership accepted the structural finding, but the reorganization was deferred behind a CEO-led redesign.

  • 35-person design org
  • Hiring criteria in use
  • Offered a direct role
Industrial B2B · New product · AI6 months

Visualization tool

Contractors needed to show homeowners how real roofing and siding would look on their house, then turn that choice straight into an order.

Associate Creative Director, Experience · Publicis Sapient

  • My part

    I led one to three designers from first research to final sprint, designing the full flow from project setup and visualization through homeowner approval to the order. The team committed early to real-time material rendering, and it never looked convincing. Contractor research showed that quality mattered more than speed, so I used Figma Make prototypes to make the case for generative-AI rendering.

  • The lesson

    I let that direction stay open too long. By the time the research and prototypes reached the client's decision-maker, much of the real-time build was done. The client held course, and the team added an AI render on top to recover quality. Next time I'll test the riskiest assumptions before the build commits.

How I work

Build shared ground before arguing structure

People who disagree about structure usually disagree about who they're serving. I get agreement on that first, then let the group reach the structure together.

Earn adoption instead of requiring it

Some of my most lasting work had no mandate behind it. I bring product and engineering in early, so a proposal reaches leadership with their support behind it. Agencies and teams adopted the work because it held up for them.

Make it testable

I prototype to put real designs in front of people early. AI speeds that up: testing interactions in Figma Make, drafting standards and documentation, exploring options before a team commits.
