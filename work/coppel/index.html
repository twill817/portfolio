Grupo Coppel

The hiring criteria I wrote shaped Coppel's senior design hires, and the principles I led are still in its design system.

Associate Creative Director, Experience · Publicis Sapient

Problem

Coppel's leadership wanted design work on par with the best tech companies, and set up a Digital Center of Excellence to get there. The gap had two halves: the craft of a 35-person design organization, and the structure around it.

Approach

I started with a skills-gap assessment against that bar, then rewrote most of the design organization's job descriptions around quality and mentoring, so leaders were craft experts with time to grow their teams, and hiring and promotion had clear criteria.

The assessment also surfaced a structural problem. Six phase-based pods each optimized their own part of the journey, and no one owned the handoffs between them. I validated that with product and technology counterparts first, so it reached leadership as a shared operating problem, then proposed organizing by channel, with a cross-channel guild owning the journey.

The work

Outcome

  • The new job descriptions were broadly agreed on, and I sat on the panel that hired senior managers against them.
  • The team wrote its own five design principles in a day-and-a-half workshop I facilitated. They're still in Coppel's design system, which credits me for leading them.
  • Leadership accepted the structural finding, but the reorganization was deferred behind a CEO-led redesign.
  • I was offered a direct role at Coppel.

My role

I joined the Digital Center of Excellence as a founding member. Over 14 months, I led the skills assessment, the job description rewrite, the operating-model proposal, and the principles workshop.
