Grupo Coppel

Customers shouldn’t be able to tell that six different teams built their experience.

Founding member of the Digital Center of Excellence, leading the skills assessment, hiring criteria, design principles and cross-functional workshops.Associate Creative Director, Experience · Publicis Sapient

Who it was for

The work was for Coppel’s customers, but most of it happened through the people building for them:

  • The designers

    35 people in six pods, each answering for one phase of the experience. I rewrote their job descriptions around craft and time to mentor.

  • The product managers

    Their personas described buyers by spend and how likely they were to finance. Nothing in them said what a customer needed or felt.

  • The customers

    People moving between the app, the website, and in-store kiosks, while no team owned the whole experience.

Problem

Coppel's leadership wanted design on par with the best tech companies. The gap was bigger than craft: product, development and six design pods each saw only their own piece of the customer's experience.

  • Senior managers were hired against my criteria, with me on the panel.

Approach

I got people to see the whole customer first, then made it stick. With my product counterpart, I brought product managers, UX designers and a developer together to move from buyer personas to customer journeys.

For the skills assessment and the team structure, there wasn't one company to copy. I drew on the teams I'd led across projects, AI research into current practice, and how development teams are usually organized.

I rewrote the design job descriptions around craft and mentoring, and brought a structural fix to leadership with product and technology already behind it.

The work

What I learned

Looking back, it was the same problem at every level: each group only saw its own piece. Getting people to see the whole moved things fastest, and the job descriptions and principles made it stick. Today I'd also write guidelines like those as files an AI coding tool can follow, so the standard goes along with the work.

My role

I was a founding member of the Digital Center of Excellence for 14 months. I led the skills assessment, job descriptions, operating-model proposal, principles workshop and mobile guidelines, and co-led the persona and journey workshops.
